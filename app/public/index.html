How the gift registry works

  1. 1
    Find something you'd like to give. Browse or search the list, or tap Available to see only what's still needed. Each gift shows how many we're hoping for and how many are already covered.
  2. 2
    Buy it from any shop. Any brand is perfect. Things like nappies, wipes, clothes, books and vouchers have no limit.
  3. 3
    Ship it straight to us. Our address is in the "Ship it to us" box on the registry, with a button to copy it. Add a gift message when you order so we know who it's from.
  4. 4
    Come back and tap "I bought this". Pick how many you bought, so everyone can see it's covered and nobody doubles up. Use your name, a funny nickname, or stay anonymous. Bought something that's not on the list? Add it under "Bought something else?" at the bottom of the registry.
  5. 5
    Tapped it by mistake? Hit Undo on the same phone or computer you used.
  6. 6
    Got $1 to spare? (optional) You can also chip in to the charity we're supporting, then write how much on the Our charity tab.

      How the winners are picked
      • Every game is worth up to 10 points.
      • Boy or girl, hair, hair colour and looks: get it right for 10 points.
      • Date, weight, length and time: start at 10 points and lose 1 for every day, 100 g, centimetre or half hour you're off.
      • Mini game bonus: save a score with the same full name as your guesses for 10 points (you only need to play once). Hold the number 1 high score when the last answer goes in for 10 more.
      • Most points overall wins first prize. If there's a tie, the closest birth date wins.
      • Each game's prize goes to the closest guess. If a few people are equally close, or everyone who picked the right answer, we'll draw one name out of the hat.
